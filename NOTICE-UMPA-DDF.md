# UMPA-DDF author fit port

`src/umpa_ddf_author_fit.cpp` and its interface port the transform and joint
optimization workflow in Smith et al. (2022), PLOS ONE 17(8), e0273315,
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0273315, supplementary S1
`single_model.py` and `utils.py`. Upstream authors retain their rights.
The upstream GNU GPL version 3 license is retained in `LICENSE-UMPA-DDF`.
This port is distributed under GPL-3.0; it is not a newly invented DDF model.
Detailed source hashes, validation and limitations: parent project
`docs/UMPA_DDF_AUTHOR_PORT.md`.
